# Concept film

A 93-second animated story about one family, one 1996 Volkswagen T4 and the idea behind Caretaker. It illustrates the hypothesis in [product-hypothesis.md](product-hypothesis.md). Nothing in it has been built, tested or verified.

- [Watch the film (MP4, 720p, 1:33)](../media/caretaker-concept-film.mp4)
- [Subtitles (SRT)](../media/caretaker-concept-film.srt)

## What the film does and does not claim

- Caretaker is shown as a concept. The film opens with "A concept film" and closes with "An open-source concept".
- The van, family, listing, service history, photos and readings are invented. The record on screen is labelled illustrative.
- Sensors appear as possible inputs, labelled "to be developed and tested".
- The concern card says "possible", shows what is not yet known and states that a photo is not a diagnosis.
- A mechanic inspects and repairs. The record changes only after a person has checked the van.
- There is no claim that Caretaker predicts failures or guarantees a safe journey.
- The environmental idea is repair and reuse. There is no claim that keeping an old vehicle is always greener than replacing it.
- No registration numbers, private records or real people appear.

## Narration

**1. The Discovery**  
Imagine finding the perfect old van. A 1996 Volkswagen T4, in Germany. Well cared for, full of character, and ready for a new adventure.

**2. The Dream**  
You bring it home to Ireland. Then you picture it: the family, the open road, and a summer drive all the way to Italy.

**3. The Question**  
But the van is thirty years old. Would you trust it to get you there? A hose you can't see. A tired belt. A connection, slowly corroding. A cooling system, working hard on a mountain road.

**4. Introducing Caretaker**  
Meet Caretaker. A companion that gets to know your machine: its history, its condition, what's been looked after, and what still needs checking.

**5. A Machine With Senses**  
It can listen to the engine. Look for changes. Read what the van's own computer can tell it. And bring together cameras, thermal images, sensors, service records, and you.

**6. From Concern To Care**  
When something changes, Caretaker helps you investigate. What it noticed, what it doesn't know yet, and what to check next. A person inspects it. The work gets done. And Caretaker remembers.

**7. The Journey**  
You're not setting off with all the answers. But you know your van better. You know what's been checked. And you have help along the way.

**8. More Life In Old Machines**  
And it doesn't stop with vans. What if the things we love had a caretaker, helping us repair them, look after them, and keep their stories going?

## How it was made

- Animation: 2D illustration drawn in code on an HTML canvas, rendered frame by frame at 1920×1080, 24 fps.
- Narration: synthetic voice from the open [Kokoro](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx) text-to-speech model (Apache-2.0). Replace with a human narrator for release, keeping the same timings.
- Music: synthesised for this film. It moves from curiosity, through uncertainty, to warmth, and ducks under the narration.
- Type: Fraunces and Inter, both under the SIL Open Font License.
