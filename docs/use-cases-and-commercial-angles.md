# Machines to keep alive: use cases and commercial angles

Caretaker starts with a T4 and aims toward a world where the machines we rely on can be maintained, repaired, restored and upgraded for much longer. This is a living opportunity list. Bring evidence, existing projects, a machine or a repair problem.

**Status:** these are ideas to test, not claims that products exist, customers will pay, or the underlying technology works for a particular machine. Observations attributed to comments are leads from the public [Jeep scanner video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WPj3aQ-xxpI), not verified findings. The idea that Ireland has a large stock of older tractors is a market hypothesis to quantify with reliable data.

| Use case | Who might pay | Problem and possible offer | Smallest external test | What could kill it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Older tractor caretaker** | Farmer, agricultural mechanic | Understand faults, service history and likely next checks for a specific tractor, especially during busy periods. Offer a mechanic-ready diagnostic and maintenance record. | Ask one Irish farmer and one independent mechanic about a recent breakdown and whether they would pay for help with a real case. | Existing tools are adequate, jobs are too infrequent, or neither sees enough value to pay. |
| **Tractor retrofit and autonomy** | Farmer, farm contractor, retrofit installer | Explore retrofitting assistance or remote operation to suitable older machinery, with more ambitious autonomy as a later research question. | Interview a farmer or installer about one repetitive task, exact model, existing retrofit choices, total cost, site constraints and desired outcome. | Existing retrofit products solve it cheaply, machine compatibility or safety constraints outweigh the value, or buyer won't pay. |
| **JCB and other construction equipment** | Owner-operator, plant hire or independent plant mechanic | Diagnose intermittent electrical and hydraulic problems and document repair history for a specific backhoe, loader or excavator. JCB is an example manufacturer, not a partner. | Ask a plant owner and mechanic for a real recent job, downtime impact and an anonymised service or fault record. | Downtime is not costly enough, the fleet is too varied, or reliable data and access are unavailable. |
| **Older ECU translator** | Specialist garage, classic-car owner, diagnostic tool maker | Make obscure diagnostics legible and provide source-linked explanations for a specific control unit. A Jeep-video commenter describes difficulty accessing tractor diagnostics; this is an unverified lead, and access varies by model. | Pick one ECU and demonstrate read-only identification and known fault/live-data output against an established tool. | Access or licence barriers, poor accuracy, or existing affordable tools already cover the unit. |
| **Intermittent fault detective** | Independent garage, owner with recurring problem | Capture symptoms and time-stamped logs during a fault, combine with workshop observations and suggest discriminating next checks. A commenter describes a long workshop investigation for a vehicle problem. | Ask a mechanic for one genuinely unresolved intermittent case and build an evidence-linked brief from logs they already have. | The brief does not save diagnostic time or fails to improve the next check. |
| **Remote co-pilot for existing scanners** | DIY owner, specialist repairer | Turn existing scanner exports, a phone, workshop photos and notes into an ongoing investigation; one commenter describes a phone-to-home-agent setup. | Ask an owner to share a de-identified existing log and compare a caretaker brief with their current workflow. | Built-in scanner software already provides comparable value or interpretation is unreliable. |
| **Whole-life machine passport** | Owners, specialist garages, small fleets | A transferable history of service, observations, parts and verified repairs that makes the next repair easier. | Manually assemble one real vehicle's history and ask owner and mechanic if it changes a decision. | Maintaining the record is more effort than its value or data quality is too low. |
| **Repair knowledge for unsupported electronics** | Owners of older TVs, electronics repairers | Investigate ways to restore useful function to otherwise unsupported devices; one commenter describes repurposing a television. | Find one repairer and one device; document a permitted restoration and working outcome. | Security, compatibility, effort or an inexpensive alternative makes it unattractive. |
| **Repairer research assistant** | Independent mechanic, mobile diesel specialist | Help search manuals and past cases, reconcile contradictory hypotheses and prepare a concise next-check plan. Several commenters offer conflicting explanations for the Jeep; their suggestions are unverified. | Ask a mechanic to evaluate a blinded brief against one completed job, then offer a paid trial on a new case. | Recommendations add noise or do not save time. |
| **Parts and component compatibility** | Specialist repairer, restorer, machine owner | Locate evidence for suitable replacement parts or alternative approaches when original components are difficult to source. | Take one documented hard-to-source component and have a specialist assess an evidence-linked shortlist. | Wrong-part risk, poor source data or existing parts networks make it uncompetitive. |
| **Workshop robot for repeatable repair tasks** | Repair business, community workshop | Eventually automate one bounded, repeatable physical task on a known fixture and document the result. | Ask a repairer to demonstrate a repetitive task and measure actual frequency and current cost before building. | Too much object variation, low frequency, integration cost or unreliable performance. |

## A sensible first bet

**Test the farmer and independent mechanic angle first.** The problem is concrete: an older tractor that needs to work, and a repairer who knows where the time goes. Ask for one recent case and an anonymised diagnostic or service record. Offer to produce a useful, evidence-linked next-check brief. If it helps, ask whether they would pay for the next one.

Treat autonomy as a separate, longer-term opportunity: first establish the machine, task, buyer, existing retrofit options and a safe test environment. Do not assume guidance assistance, remote operation and full autonomy are interchangeable.

## How to add an angle

Open a [machine or project issue](https://github.com/Hulupeep/caretaker/issues/new?template=machine.yml), share an [experiment](https://github.com/Hulupeep/caretaker/issues/new?template=experiment.yml), or submit a pull request. Describe the machine, the person with the problem, the evidence and what would change your mind. Link original sources and credit creators.
